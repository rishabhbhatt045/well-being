What Does "Confidential" Actually Mean in Online Therapy?

Confidentiality is the professional promise that whatever you share with your therapist — your thoughts, your history, your struggles — stays between the two of you. This isn't just a nice-to-have. In India, it's grounded in professional ethics codes and, since the Mental Healthcare Act of 2017, backed by law.

The good news: this promise doesn't weaken just because your session happens over video instead of in person. A licensed therapist is bound by the same duty of confidentiality whether you're sitting across a desk or logging in from your bedroom.

Common Privacy Worries Indian Clients Have

If you've hesitated to book a session because of these thoughts, you're in good company. Here's the honest answer to each one.

"What if my family finds out?"

Sessions happen through a private login, with headphones and a closed door being your only real safeguard on your end. The platform itself never notifies anyone in your life that you're in therapy.

"Will this show up on my employment or insurance records?"

No. Mental health platforms are not connected to employer HR systems or insurance databases unless you're using a corporate wellness benefit and choose to submit a claim yourself.

"Can my therapist talk about me to another therapist?"

Only in rare, structured clinical supervision, and even then your identity is typically anonymised. This is standard practice, not a privacy breach.

"Is video counselling actually secure, technically?"

Reputable platforms use end-to-end encrypted, HIPAA-aligned video infrastructure — the same standard used in secure telemedicine, not a regular video-calling app.

When Can Confidentiality Legally Be Broken?

Confidentiality isn't absolute, and a good therapist will tell you this upfront rather than hide it. The exceptions are narrow, and they exist to keep you and others safe:

  • There is a serious, immediate risk of harm to yourself or someone else.
  • A child or vulnerable adult is at risk of abuse.
  • A court of law formally orders disclosure of records.

Outside of these situations, your therapist cannot legally or ethically share what you tell them — not with your parents, your partner, or your employer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online therapy as confidential as in-person therapy?

Yes. The same professional and legal confidentiality rules apply regardless of format. Encrypted platforms often add an extra layer of technical security that a face-to-face office cannot offer.

Can my family access my therapy details on BetterLYF?

No. Your account, session content, and notes are accessible only to you and your assigned therapist. Family members have no visibility into your therapy unless you choose to share it yourself.

What happens to my data if I stop therapy?

Your records remain securely stored under the platform's data retention policy and are not shared or sold. You can typically request more detail on retention and deletion directly from your provider.

Is online counselling safe for Indian users specifically?

Yes, provided the platform employs licensed therapists and follows India's data protection and mental health regulations. Checking for these credentials before signing up is always a smart first step.

Will using online therapy affect my job or visa applications?

No. Therapy records are private health information and are not accessible to employers, embassies, or background-check agencies under normal circumstances.
