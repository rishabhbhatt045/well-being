What Is Karma? Understanding the Meaning Behind the Quotes

Karma is often misunderstood as punishment or reward. In reality, it refers to the principle of cause and effect — actions creating consequences.

Simple Explanation of Karma

  • Thoughts influence behavior
  • Behavior creates outcomes
  • Outcomes shape future experiences

Karma is less about fate and more about responsibility. Modern psychology also supports this idea, showing how mindset and actions influence emotional health.
