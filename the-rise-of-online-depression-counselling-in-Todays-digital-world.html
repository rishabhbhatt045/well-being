Common formats include:

  • Chat-based counselling

Signs You Might Benefit From Depression Counselling

Depression often develops gradually, making it difficult to recognize.

  • Persistent sadness or hopelessness
  • Loss of interest in activities once enjoyed
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Changes in sleep or appetite
  • Low energy and fatigue
  • Feelings of worthlessness or guilt

If these symptoms continue for weeks, professional depression counselling can help individuals understand and manage their emotions.
