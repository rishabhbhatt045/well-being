The Connection Between Marriage and Emotional Wellness

Research consistently shows that healthy relationships contribute to emotional well-being. When couples feel understood, supported, and valued, they often experience lower stress levels and higher life satisfaction. Emotional wellness in marriage involves self-awareness, empathy, healthy boundaries, and constructive communication.

Romantic relationships are not just about affection. They also provide emotional security, encouragement, and companionship. Couples who prioritize emotional wellness often report stronger satisfaction and greater resilience during difficult times.
