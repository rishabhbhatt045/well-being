When Quotes Aren’t Enough: Recognizing Deeper Signs

While depression quotes offer comfort, persistent symptoms may require professional help.

  • Continuous sadness
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Low energy or motivation
  • Feelings of worthlessness

If these signs last more than two weeks, seeking counselling is recommended.
