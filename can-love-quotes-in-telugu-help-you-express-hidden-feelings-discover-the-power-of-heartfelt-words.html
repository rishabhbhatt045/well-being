Before sharing a quote, identify what you're truly feeling. Are you expressing admiration, affection, gratitude, longing, or commitment?

Avoid sharing quotes simply because they sound beautiful. Choose one that genuinely mirrors your emotions and situation.

Adding a personal touch makes a quote even more meaningful.

Step 4: Choose the Right Moment
