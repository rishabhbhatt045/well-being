✨ The One Simple Couple Counselling Trick

The Trick: "Reflective Listening"
Instead of reacting immediately, repeat what your partner said in your own words before responding.

This technique is widely used in professional couple counselling because it reduces misunderstandings and builds emotional safety.

---

🪜 Step-by-Step Guide to Use This Trick

1. Pause Before Reacting

Take a breath. Don’t interrupt or jump to conclusions.

2. Listen Fully

Focus on your partner’s words, tone, and emotions.

3. Reflect Back

Say something like: “So what you’re saying is…”

4. Validate Feelings

Even if you disagree, acknowledge their emotions.

5. Respond Calmly

Now share your perspective without blame.

---

❤️ Why This Trick Works So Well

  • Prevents misunderstandings instantly
  • Makes your partner feel heard and valued
  • Reduces emotional escalation
  • Builds trust and connection
  • Encourages respectful communication

BetterLyf experts often recommend this technique during online counselling sessions because of its simplicity and effectiveness.

---

📊 Real-Life Example

Instead of:
“You never listen to me!”

Try:
“I hear that you feel ignored when I don’t respond properly. Is that right?”

See the difference? One creates conflict. The other creates understanding.
