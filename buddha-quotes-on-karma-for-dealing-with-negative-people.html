Understanding Karma in Simple Words

Karma means that every action has a consequence. What you give to the world eventually comes back to you.

  • Positive actions → Positive outcomes
  • Negative actions → Negative consequences
  • Intent matters as much as action

When dealing with negative people, karma reminds us to stay grounded and not absorb their energy.

Powerful Buddha Quotes on Karma

“Holding onto anger is like drinking poison and expecting the other person to suffer.”
“What you think, you become. What you feel, you attract.”
“Your worst enemy cannot harm you as much as your own thoughts.”
“If you truly loved yourself, you could never hurt another.”

These karma quotes in English highlight the importance of self-awareness and emotional balance.

Why Negative People Affect You So Much

Negative people often project their insecurities onto others. Without realizing it, you may absorb their stress, anger, or pessimism.

Common Signs of Negative People

  • Constant complaining
  • Blaming others
  • Draining your energy
  • Spreading negativity

Recognizing these signs is the first step toward protecting your mental health.

How Karma Helps You Deal with Negative People

1. Focus on Your Actions

Karma teaches you to control your reactions. Stay calm and composed, even when others aren’t.

2. Set Healthy Boundaries

Not everyone deserves access to your energy. It’s okay to distance yourself.

3. Let Karma Do Its Work

You don’t need revenge. Life has its own way of balancing things.

4. Choose Positivity

Respond with kindness, not negativity. This shifts your energy and protects your peace.
